What this forecast is — and isn't

Every number above comes only from SRMT's order dates and quantities in the POs supplied. For each code, "avg gap" is the average number of days between that code's appearances on a PO, and "forecast qty" is a blend of its last 3 order quantities and its trend line. That's enough to flag which codes are due and roughly how much — it is not yet a true demand forecast, because it has no visibility into SRMT's actual consumption, your own stock on hand, or your lead time to replenish raw material. The requirements below close that gap.

What's left to make this fully accurate

1. Reel yield (sheets per reel)

I know which reel width/GSM each item draws from, but not how many sheets one 90 cm-dia reel actually yields at that width & GSM. With that I can turn "SRMT/54 forecast: 20,000 pcs" into "that's ~2.1 reels of 43" 120 GSM" instead of just flagging the reel to watch.

2. The unmatched items

Mostly the metal-hardware codes (king pins, bell-crank pins, coupling flanges) — your board-size file didn't cover these. If they also ship in a corrugated tray/box, send their sheet size and I'll add them to the reel guide.

3. Supplier lead times

Days from placing a reel order to it arriving at your dock. This turns "reel due in 29 days" into "order the reel by <date>."

4. Safety stock policy

A buffer rule (e.g. "always hold 2 reels of your top 5 widths") to protect against SRMT ordering early or in a larger batch than history suggests.

5. Any known seasonality or program changes

Do certain codes only ship in some months? Is SRMT planning volume changes, new codes, or discontinuations? History alone can't see this.

6. A live feed instead of PDFs

An email inbox, shared folder, or ERP export that drops each new PO in automatically would let this dashboard update itself instead of being re-built by hand.

Source: purchase orders from Sri Ramadas Motor Transport Ltd (Kakinada) to M/s. Sunanda Packaging Industries, as supplied. All quantities in EA. "Forecast qty" and "next PO due" are statistical projections from order history only — not a guarantee of what SRMT will actually order.
